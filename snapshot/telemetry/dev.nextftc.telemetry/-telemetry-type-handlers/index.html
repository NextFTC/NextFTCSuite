TelemetryTypeHandlers

class TelemetryTypeHandlers(source)

The TelemetryTypeHandlers of a TelemetryRegistry, by the type they handle.

A handler applies to its type and to every subtype. For a value, the handler registered for the nearest type wins: its own class, then its superclasses from the closest, then its interfaces. A handler beats the value's own TelemetryLoggable, like a Comparator beats natural ordering, but not the types that always have a fixed encoding, such as numbers, strings and primitive arrays.

Handlers for Measure are registered by default.
